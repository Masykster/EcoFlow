# EcoFlow

Product that turns financial activity into carbon insights with a guest calculator, auth dashboard, and history.

## Language

**EcoFlow**:
The product as a whole (Laravel + Livewire app with Astro landing).
_Avoid_: EcoStep as product name, laravel/livewire-starter-kit

**EcoStep**:
The calculator / dashboard / history feature inside EcoFlow that estimates CO2e.
_Avoid_: standalone app, product name

**Landing**:
The public marketing page generated from Astro into a Laravel Blade view.
_Avoid_: Astro app, separate frontend
